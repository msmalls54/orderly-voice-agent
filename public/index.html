LIVE PROTOTYPE

Talk to Orderly

Clear conversational voice

Try saying: “Order twelve regular donut holes and two apple fritters from My Happy Donut.”

The microphone starts only after you choose to begin.

01

Call and ask

Say what you want naturally. Orderly keeps the conversation focused and easy to follow.

02

Exact read-back

Orderly reads every item, tip, delivery address, masked payment method, and total aloud.

03

Spoken confirmation

After the full read-back, a clear spoken “yes” confirms only that active checkout. Any change requires a new read-back.
