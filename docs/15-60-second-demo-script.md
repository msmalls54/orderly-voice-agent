# Orderly — 60-second demo

## Stage setup

- Keep purchasing locked for the stage call.
- Put the phone on speaker with volume at maximum.
- Start on the problem slide; make the proof slide with the sanitized transcript and real receipt the very next slide.
- Do not explain the architecture before dialing.

## Exact one-minute flow

| Time | Speaker | Words / action |
|---:|---|---|
| 0:00–0:07 | Presenter | “Food-delivery apps can be impossible for older adults to navigate. Orderly replaces the entire screen with one normal phone call.” |
| 0:07–0:11 | Presenter | “I’m not opening DoorDash. I’m calling Orderly.” Dial and use speakerphone. |
| 0:11–0:18 | Caller | After the greeting: “Get me 12 donut holes and two apple fritters from My Happy Donut.” |
| 0:18–0:38 | Orderly | Let Orderly finish the live cart and total readback. Do not interrupt it. |
| 0:38–0:40 | Caller | After the full readback: “Yes.” |
| 0:40–0:47 | Orderly | It confirms the locked rehearsal. Do not repeat “yes,” redial, or troubleshoot onstage. |
| 0:47–1:00 | Presenter | Advance to the proof slide: “That same voice flow placed this real DoorDash order earlier today: one approval, one submission, zero retries, and zero duplicates. AgentPhone carries the call, ElevenLabs is the voice, and Orderly safely controls DoorDash.” |

## One-line fallback

If the call is not at the readback by 0:35, hang up, show the proof slide, and say: “Venue connectivity stalled, so here is the same flow completed live earlier today—one call, one approval, and one real DoorDash order.”

## Closing phrase if one second remains

“For the customer, the phone call is the entire app.”
